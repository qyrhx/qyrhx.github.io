hrsir: RSS feed reader

source code: github.com/qyrhx/hrsir

Overview

A small RSS reader in Haskell. I built it after working through many small problems, such as Advent of Code, in the language. It was my first project where I did some real design, and I had to do it the Haskell way, which is quite different from the object-oriented or data-driven mindset I was used to.
